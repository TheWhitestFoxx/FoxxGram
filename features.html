Why FoxxGram?

Simple

FoxxGram is so straightforward you already know how to use it.

Private

FoxxGram messages are locked down and can vanish when you choose.

Synced

FoxxGram keeps the same chats on your phone, tablet, and computer.

Fast

FoxxGram is built to deliver messages in an instant.

Powerful

FoxxGram does not get in the way of large files or long histories.

Open

FoxxGram publishes an API and source so others can study how it works.

Secure

FoxxGram is designed to keep your messages safe from attackers.

Social

FoxxGram groups can hold up to 200,000 members.

Expressive

FoxxGram lets you customize how your messenger looks and feels.
