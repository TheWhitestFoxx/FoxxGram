General Questions

Q: What is FoxxGram? What do I do here?

FoxxGram is a messaging app built for speed and privacy. It is free to use and works on all of your devices at once, so the same chats stay in sync on phones, tablets, computers, and even a watch. You can send messages, photos, videos, and files, start large groups, or broadcast with a channel. Voice and video calls are part of the same app.

Q: Who is it for?

Anyone who wants a simple place for personal or work conversations. If you need one app that can handle a quiet one-to-one chat and a very large community, FoxxGram is aimed at you.

Q: How is it different from other messengers?

FoxxGram keeps your cloud chats available on several devices at the same time, supports very large groups, and offers optional secret chats with a timer. There is also an open API so other tools can be built on the same network.

Q: Is it available on my device?

Official apps cover Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows, macOS, Linux, and modern web browsers. See the apps page for the full list.

Q: How do you make money?

This demo describes a product that would stay free for everyday messaging, with optional extras for people who want more storage, tools, or appearance options. No payment form is included on this site.

Q: What are your thoughts on internet privacy?

Private conversation should be the default, not a hidden setting. FoxxGram is described here as a service that collects only what it needs to deliver messages and gives you controls over who can find you.

FoxxGram Basics

Q: Who can I message?

People in your contacts who also use FoxxGram, plus anyone who shares a username or invite link with you. You can also write to public channels and bots.

Q: What do the check marks mean?

One check means the message left your device. Two checks mean it was delivered. When the other person opens it, the checks can change color to show that it was read, unless they have turned that off.

Q: Can I hide my last seen status?

Yes. You can limit last seen and online status to everyone, contacts, or nobody. If you hide yours, you will not see the exact status of people who also hide theirs.

Q: Can I delete my messages?

Yes. You can delete a message just for yourself or, in most chats, for everyone. Secret chats can also expire on a timer.

Q: What about voice, video, and group calls?

You can place one-to-one voice and video calls, and start group calls that people can join when they are ready. Group voice chats can stay open so members drop in and out.

Groups and Channels

Q: What makes FoxxGram groups useful?

Groups can grow to 200,000 members, keep a shared history, and add tools like topics, bots, and admin roles. That makes them useful for both friends and large communities.

Q: How are channels and groups different?

Groups are for conversation among members. Channels are for broadcasting: a smaller set of people posts, and a much larger audience can follow along.

Q: How do I add members?

You can add people from your contacts or share an invite link. Admins can revoke a link or switch to approval for new members.

Usernames

Q: What are usernames? How do I get one?

A username is a short public name, like @yourname, that people can use to find you without seeing your phone number. You set it in the app settings if the name is still free.

Q: Do I need a username?

No. You can keep using FoxxGram with only your phone number. A username is helpful if you want to be found without sharing that number.

Q: Will people know my number?

People who already have your number in their contacts may still see it. You can limit who sees your number in the privacy settings, and a username lets strangers reach you without it.

Security

Q: How secure is FoxxGram?

Cloud chats are encrypted in transit and stored so you can open them on every device. Secret chats use end-to-end encryption on the two devices in that conversation. You can also turn on two-step verification.

Q: How do you encrypt data?

In this design, everyday chats are encrypted between your apps and the service, and secret chats are encrypted on the devices themselves so only those devices can read them.

Q: How does two-step verification work?

After you enter the login code sent to your device, FoxxGram can also ask for a password you chose. That extra step helps if someone else gets hold of a login code.

Secret Chats

Q: What is a secret chat?

A secret chat lives only on the two devices that started it. It is end-to-end encrypted, does not sync to your other devices, and can use a self-destruct timer.

Q: How does the self-destruct timer work?

You set a timer on the chat or on a single message. After the other person reads it, the message disappears once the timer runs out.

Q: Why not make every chat a secret chat?

Secret chats cannot follow you to a new phone or a desktop at the same time. Cloud chats exist so you can pick up the same conversation on any of your devices.

Your Account

Q: Who can see my phone number?

You control this in privacy settings. You can show it to everybody, only your contacts, or nobody. People who already saved your number may still see that they have it.

Q: I have a new phone number. What do I do?

Open settings and change the number on your account. You will need access to the new number so the app can confirm it.

Q: How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from the app settings. After that, your cloud messages and profile are removed from the service. This website does not include a login or deletion form.

Bots

Q: How do I create a bot?

You create a bot through the in-app bot father tool, then use the API from the apps and platform pages to give it behavior. This demo does not include a developer console.

Q: What are mini apps?

Mini apps are small programs that open inside a chat, such as a shop, a form, or a game, without leaving FoxxGram.

Deeper Questions

Q: Can I use the FoxxGram API?

Yes. The platform is described as having a public API and a client library so other apps can be built on the same network. See the library section.

Q: Can I help?

If you write code, you can review the published apps, suggest fixes, or build a community client. If you speak another language, translation help is always useful.

Troubleshooting

Q: I am not getting a login code.

Check that you typed the number correctly, wait a minute, and try the option to receive the code in an existing FoxxGram session if you are already logged in on another device. This site cannot send codes or reset accounts.

Q: Notifications are missing.

Make sure system notifications are allowed for FoxxGram, that you are not in a muted chat, and that battery savers are not stopping the app from waking up.

Support

For anything else, the in-app support chat is the right place to ask. This website is a static design demo and cannot receive support messages, logins, or passwords.

FoxxGram will never ask you for your two-step password or login codes on this website. There is no sign-in form here.
